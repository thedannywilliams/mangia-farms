The Experience

An abundant Italian table.

Mangia Farms creates bespoke edible tablescapes for intimate dinners, weddings, showers, celebrations and luxury events throughout Los Angeles.

Every tablescape is built around our handmade breads, focaccia, crackers, cultured butters, tapenades, dips and seasonal accompaniments, all prepared in-house using generational Italian family recipes. Beautiful cheeses, fresh fruit, florals and thoughtfully sourced Italian provisions complete each installation.

Made for Gathering

More than a grazing table.

Every Mangia Farms tablescape is a one-of-a-kind edible experience, rooted in family tradition, designed with intention and made for gathering.

Custom boards, intimate tablescapes and full-scale event installations available.
